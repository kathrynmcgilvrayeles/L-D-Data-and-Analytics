L&D AI Data Lexicon

Use these analytical terms and action verbs in your prompts to direct Generative AI to evaluate learning and performance data with precision.

High-Impact Analytical Verbs

  • "Correlate..."

    Identifies the relationship between two or more variables (e.g., "Does completion of the consultative selling pathway correlate with higher quarterly client retention?").

  • "Identify outliers..."

    Highlights data points that deviate significantly from the norm (e.g., "Which business units have unusually high drop-off rates in Module 3 of the onboarding programme?").

  • "Synthesise the trend..."

    Extracts the overarching trajectory or pattern across longitudinal data (e.g., capability assessment growth over 12 months).

Foundational Spreadsheet Functions & AI

Before feeding data into Generative AI—or when verifying AI-generated outputs—it helps to understand how core spreadsheet functions aggregate learning metrics. COUNTIF and SUMIF are essential building blocks for summarising raw LMS spreadsheets.

COUNTIF

The COUNTIF function tallies how many times a specific value or text string appears in a dataset based on a single criterion. In L&D, this is ideal for counting how many participants have a status of "Completed", how many learners selected a specific distractor in a multiple-choice quiz, or how many times a department appears in an attendance log.

SUMIF

The SUMIF function totals numerical values across a range based on a specified criterion. For example, you can calculate the total CPD hours logged by a specific business unit, or compare total formative versus summative assessment points earned across a cohort. Because SUMIF evaluates one criterion at a time, you can ask AI to write nested formulas (like SUMIFS) when filtering by multiple variables.

Practical L&D Data Use Cases

Explore how L&D professionals across industries use natural language prompts to turn everyday learning and operational data into strategic business insights.

1. Linking Learning to Risk & Quality Outcomes

Scenario: Evaluating whether completion of a refreshed compliance or quality assurance module correlates with a reduction in operational errors or WHS incidents.

"Analyse the attached Q2 WHS capability completion dataset alongside the quarterly workplace incident log. Correlate module completion rates and assessment scores with incident frequency across business units. Present the findings in a summary table and highlight any departments where high completion is not translating to safer workplace behaviour."

2. Accelerating Onboarding Time-to-Proficiency

Scenario: Measuring how quickly new starters transition from onboarding completion to meeting core role KPIs across different hybrid and regional cohorts.

"Compare the onboarding cohort start dates with the dates new starters first achieved their baseline performance KPIs in this spreadsheet. Calculate the average time-to-proficiency across different functions and delivery modes (in-person vs virtual), and identify any statistically significant outliers."

3. Thematic Synthesis of Qualitative Evaluations

Scenario: Extracting actionable instructional design improvements from hundreds of open-text post-programme survey responses.

"Review the attached spreadsheet containing 450 open-text participant evaluations from our Leadership Foundations programme. Conduct a thematic sentiment analysis, grouping feedback into 'Key Enablers of Learning Transfer' and 'Barriers to Application'. Provide a percentage breakdown by theme and include two representative participant quotes for each."
